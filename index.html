      06 — FAQ

      Questions people ask us every day.

      What are your store timings?

      We're open 9:00 AM to 7:30 PM, all 7 days of the week — Sundays included. There's no weekly off.

      Do you deliver outside Chhal?

      Yes — we deliver across Chhal and nearby areas of Raigarh district. Call us with your village or locality and we'll confirm delivery for big appliances.

      Can I buy on EMI?

      EMI options are available on select products. Ask us in-store or on the phone — we'll explain the plans, tenure and documents needed in simple terms.

      Do you take old appliances in exchange?

      Yes. TVs, refrigerators, washing machines and more can be exchanged depending on condition. Bring photos on WhatsApp or visit the shop for a quick valuation.

      Will you help with installation and repairs?

      Absolutely — our team handles installation (AC fitting, TV mounting, etc.) and we guide you through warranty service and repairs after purchase.

      Are the products genuine with warranty?

      Every product we sell is genuine, sealed, billed with GST, and covered by the brand's official warranty. That's a promise we've kept for over 15 years.
